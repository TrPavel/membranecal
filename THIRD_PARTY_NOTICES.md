# Third-party attribution and permission scope

OPM: Lomize MA, Pogozheva ID, Joo H, Mosberg HI, Lomize AL. OPM database and PPM web server: resources for positioning of proteins in membranes. Nucleic Acids Research. 2012;40:D370-D376. https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkr703 . Source: https://opm.phar.umich.edu/ .

Explicit permission from Andrei Lomize was reported by the author for redistributing the numerical OPM values described in the author request for this MembraneCal reproducibility package, including required membrane orientation/depth/thickness information. Attribution and transformation provenance are retained. Unrelated raw OPM assets and arbitrary database redistribution are outside this permission. The message date and original email headers were not supplied; none are invented. No private correspondence is redistributed.

PDB structures, SIFTS mappings, UniProt identifiers/sequences and AlphaFold Database predictions retain their source-specific terms and citations in the manuscript and retained source ledger. Raw source archives are not included. Derived numerical inputs preserve source URL/hash and transformation provenance. Official Google DeepMind lddt.py retains its upstream Apache-2.0 copyright notice. Software dependencies are installed separately and retain their respective licences.

  https://alphafold.ebi.ac.uk/assets/License-Disclaimer.pdf

  https://www.ebi.ac.uk/about/terms-of-use/

  licence evidence is retained as history, not generalized into unlimited asset rights.

The numerical dependency lock installs upstream packages separately. Arial fonts
are a reference-rendering prerequisite and are not redistributed. The embedded
official lDDT implementation retains its original Apache-2.0 notice.
