# Community conduct

Discuss evidence and methods respectfully. Welcome researchers with different
backgrounds, experience and interpretations. Critique claims and code rather than
people. Harassment, discrimination, threats and disclosure of private information
are not acceptable.

Report concerns privately to trofimchikpavel@icloud.com. The maintainer may edit or
remove inappropriate content and restrict participation, applying proportionate
actions and explaining decisions where privacy permits. Disagreement about a
scientific result is not itself misconduct.
